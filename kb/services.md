## Full truckload
Dedicated trucks for a single customer, mostly from the Dallas hub. Typical transit within Texas is one day. Quotes come from the sales team and are valid for 14 days.

## Less than truckload
Shared trucks for smaller shipments, consolidated at Dallas and Houston. Pickups must be booked by 2 PM the day before.

## Warehousing and fulfillment
Storage, pick-and-pack and order fulfillment for retail and e-commerce customers. Houston offers temperature-controlled storage between 2 and 8 degrees Celsius for food and pharmacy customers. Phoenix handles same-day e-commerce fulfillment for orders received by noon.

## Last-mile delivery
Home and business delivery within 60 miles of the Phoenix and Dallas hubs, with photo proof of delivery.

## Harbor Track portal
Harbor Track is our customer portal. Customers use it to book shipments, track trucks live, download invoices and open support tickets. Version 2 adds delivery time predictions and is planned for the end of Q3.
