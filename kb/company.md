## About the company
Harbor Point Logistics is a regional freight and warehousing company founded in 2014. We move and store goods for mid-sized retailers, food producers and electronics brands across the southern United States. We have about 240 employees.

## Mission
Our mission is to make freight boringly reliable: every shipment on time, every customer informed, no surprises.

## Locations
- Headquarters: Austin, Texas. Leadership, sales, finance, IT and customer support work here.
- Dallas warehouse: our largest site, 180,000 square feet, cross-docking and full truckload.
- Houston warehouse: food-grade and temperature-controlled storage.
- Phoenix warehouse: e-commerce fulfillment and last-mile delivery hub, opened in 2023.

## Office hours
Headquarters is open Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 6 PM Central Time. Warehouses run two shifts, 6 AM to 10 PM, Monday to Saturday. The operations control desk is staffed 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

## Company goals for this year
- On-time delivery rate of 96 percent or better every month.
- Grow revenue 15 percent year over year, mainly through warehousing and fulfillment.
- Cut average support ticket resolution time below 8 hours.
- Launch version 2 of the Harbor Track customer portal by the end of Q3.

## Key numbers
- Fleet: 85 owned trucks plus contracted carriers.
- Customers: about 120 active business customers.
- Average monthly revenue: about 4.2 million dollars.
