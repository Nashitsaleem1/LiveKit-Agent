# Company policies

## Paid time off
Full-time employees get 20 days of paid time off per year, plus 10 public holidays. Request time off in the HR portal at least two weeks ahead. Up to 5 unused days carry over to the next year.

## Sick leave
Employees get 8 paid sick days per year. Tell your manager before your shift starts. A doctor's note is needed for absences longer than 3 days.

## Remote work
Office staff at headquarters may work from home up to two days a week, agreed with their manager. Warehouse and control desk roles are on site.

## Expenses
- Expenses up to 500 dollars are approved by your manager.
- Expenses from 500 to 2,000 dollars need your department head.
- Expenses above 2,000 dollars need the Chief Financial Officer, Priya Raman.
- Submit receipts in the finance portal within 30 days.

## Business travel
Book flights and hotels through the finance team. Economy class for flights under 6 hours. The daily meal allowance is 60 dollars.

## IT security
- Multi-factor authentication is required on all company accounts.
- Use the company VPN on any network outside our offices.
- Never share passwords, including with IT staff.
- Report suspicious emails to the IT help desk right away.
- Customer data must not be stored on personal devices.

## Safety in warehouses
High-visibility vests and safety shoes are required on all warehouse floors. Forklifts may only be driven by certified staff. Report any incident to the shift lead within one hour.
