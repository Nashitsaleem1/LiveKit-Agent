# Frequently asked questions

> Made-up sample data. Replace with real content.

## When is payday?
Employees are paid twice a month, on the 15th and on the last working day of the month.

## How do I reset my password?
Use the self-service reset page on the intranet, or call the IT help desk at extension 4100. IT will never ask you for your password.

## How do I connect to the VPN?
Open the Harbor Point VPN app on your company laptop and sign in with your company account and multi-factor code. If it fails, call the IT help desk.

## What counts as an on-time delivery?
A delivery is on time if it arrives within the agreed delivery window, or no more than 30 minutes after it for full truckload.

## What happens when a shipment is delayed?
The operations control desk contacts the customer within 30 minutes of a known delay, logs the reason, and gives a new arrival time. Delays longer than 4 hours are escalated to the warehouse manager.

## Where is the Wi-Fi password?
Guest Wi-Fi details are posted at each reception desk. Staff laptops connect to the staff network automatically.

## Is there parking at headquarters?
Yes, free parking in the garage behind the Austin office. Visitor spaces are on level one.

## How do I book a meeting room?
Meeting rooms are booked through the shared company calendar. The large boardroom at headquarters seats 16 people.

## Who approves a new customer contract?
Contracts are prepared by sales, checked by finance, and signed by the Head of Sales. Contracts above 500,000 dollars a year also need the Chief Executive Officer.
